Please fix the Staff Overview button in `src/pages/AdminDashboard.jsx`. The goal is simple: add a **Staff Overview button next to the + New Ticket button**, and when clicked it opens a full-screen modal showing `<StaffOverviewPage />`.

Delete the `ticketsSubTab` state and the sub-tab pill navigation (the `<div>` containing the "All Tickets" / "Staff Overview" toggle buttons). Also remove the `{ticketsSubTab === 'staff-overview' ? <StaffOverviewPage /> : ...}` conditional — just show the tickets list directly.

Add this state near the top of the component (with the other useState declarations):
